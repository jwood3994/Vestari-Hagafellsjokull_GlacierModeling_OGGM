My undergraduate dissertation, of which I used a popular dynamic glacier model to track current glacier melt patterns in the past 20 years and predict future decline of a specific outlet glacier and assess its feasibility.

The research objectives were as followed:

1. Identify patterns of mass balance of Vestari-Hagafellsjökull glacier in Iceland (A major outlet glacier on the large Langjökul ice cap) in the past 20
years and test OGGM derived mass balance to in-situ mass balance records.

2. Assess potential hydrological changes simulated by OGGM that indicate the glacier may be in an retreat state.
   
3. Create future projections of the glacier for the year 2100 using different climate models and to asses area, volume and run-off patterns simulated by the model.
